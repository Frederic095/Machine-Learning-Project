## Context
The Indian-based pharmaceutical company HumanForYou employs approximately 4,000 people. However, each year it experiences a turnover rate of around 15% of its employees, requiring it to find similar profiles on the job market.

Management believes that this level of turnover is not good for the company because:

* The projects on which the employees leaving the company were working fall behind schedule, which damages the company's reputation with its customers and partners.

* A large human resources department must be maintained in order to have the resources to find new recruits.
  
* Time is lost when new employees arrive because they often need training and time to become fully operational in their new environment. Management therefore calls on us to determine the factors that have the greatest influence on this turnover rate and to propose models that will provide avenues for improvement to make their employees want to stay.
## Problem Statement
What factors are responsible for employee attrition in the company? By performing exploratory data analysis (EDA), we can find out how each characteristic is responsible for employee attrition in the company, and then by designing ML models, find the probability of characteristics for attrition.

## Objective
We need to model the probability of attrition using an algorithm. The results obtained will be used by management to understand what changes it should make to its workplace in order to encourage most of its employees to stay.
